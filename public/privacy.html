Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Nerdi, LLC

DotCloser is operated by Nerdi, LLC. We store your sign-in identity, seller profile, domain portfolio, buyer contacts, email drafts, subscription status, and sending records to provide the service.

Google and Microsoft authorization is used to send the messages you approve. DotCloser does not read your inbox. Authorization tokens are encrypted at rest. You may disconnect a mailbox in Settings, which removes its saved token and cancels queued sends.

Stripe processes payments. DotCloser does not store payment card numbers. Google, Meta, Microsoft, hosting providers, and configured research providers process data needed for their respective services.

Google user data is used only for the requested email-sending features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. DotCloser’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

We retain account data while the account is active and as needed for billing, security and suppression records. Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing privacy@dotcloser.com from your account email.
