Account & data deletion

Effective September 28, 2026 · Nerdi, LLC

To request account and personal data deletion, email privacy@dotcloser.com from your account email with the subject “Delete my DotCloser account.” We may verify the request before deleting data.

You can immediately remove mailbox access through Settings → Connect your email → Disconnect. You can also revoke DotCloser through your Google, Facebook, or Microsoft account settings.

After verification, your account, linked identities, saved domains, prospects and drafts will be deleted. Billing and anti-abuse records may be retained when legally required; suppression records may be retained to honor opt-outs.
